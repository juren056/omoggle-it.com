Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026.

This policy describes the current Omoggle IT code. Omoggle IT is independent and is not affiliated with Omoggle LLC.

Free local photo and camera tools

The free tools at /tools, /psl-test, /mog-score, /face-shape-detector and /omoggle-practice-test decode the selected image or camera frame in your browser. The image pixels and detected facial landmarks are not sent to our application server, analytics provider or advertising provider. We do not create a public result URL or save those inputs.

Your browser downloads the site’s self-hosted MediaPipe model and WebAssembly files. Those ordinary file requests are different from uploading your photo. MediaPipe’s package notice says its Tasks APIs may send performance and utilization metrics to Google; it also says input processing occurs on device and input data is not sent to Google. Clearing or leaving a tool releases its preview and processing resources.

Legacy paid cloud analysis

An older cloud-analysis API remains available only to signed-in users whose active paid entitlement can be verified, and the owner can disable it with a server-side switch. When a legacy member deliberately uses that feature, the image is sent through our server to the configured GPTSAPI vision service for analysis. The current application code does not write the image to Supabase or object storage, but third-party processing is still involved. Do not use the legacy feature unless you accept that transfer.

Accounts and billing

Clerk handles sign-in identity. Supabase stores account-related entitlement, quota and subscription records. Stripe handles checkout, billing management and cancellation for existing subscriptions. We do not receive full payment-card numbers. Retention and deletion requests may be constrained by security, tax and transaction-record obligations.

Advertising and analytics

The site does not display a cookie or optional-services popup. Google Analytics is disabled in the current application. If the owner enables Adsterra with valid production configuration, eligible public content pages may load a clearly labelled native or display placement. Advertising scripts are not loaded on photo, camera, account, billing, sign-in or sign-up routes.

Tool events are designed not to include images, Base64 data, landmarks, filenames, exact face scores or form text. Advertising is controlled by a site-wide kill switch and may be fully disabled. The site does not use popunders, forced redirects or ads that unlock results.

Your choices

You can block third-party advertising requests through your browser or content blocker; core content and local tools continue working. Review Adsterra’s own privacy information for any storage or identifiers used by an enabled placement.

Data minimization and security

Do not submit another person’s photo without permission. The tools are limited to the user or an authorized adult. We do not intentionally infer race, health, intelligence or identity. No internet service can promise absolute security, but the free default avoids creating a server-side photo copy.

Contact and requests

Use the contact page for access or deletion requests. Include the account email only when needed; never email a face photo, password or payment-card details.
